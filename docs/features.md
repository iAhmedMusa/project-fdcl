# FDCL Platform Features

A plain-language breakdown of every feature available to customers, staff, and admins on the Focus Digital Color Lab platform.

---

## For Customers

### Creating an Account
Customers can sign up with their email address or log in with their Google account. After registering, they get access to their personal dashboard where all orders and appointments are tracked.

New accounts require phone number verification via a one-time code (OTP) sent by SMS. This keeps accounts secure and ensures FDCL can contact customers when their order is ready.

### Booking a Studio Appointment
Customers can book a photo session online without calling or visiting first. The booking form asks for:
- Which studio location (Bailey Road or Gulshan)
- What type of photo is needed (passport, visa, school, family, etc.)
- A preferred date and time slot (30-minute slots from 9:00 AM to 9:30 PM; Fridays from 3:00 PM)
- Customer name and contact number

The system automatically blocks out past time slots and adjusts Friday hours.

### Ordering Products Online
From the customer dashboard, orders can be placed for:

**Photo Album**
Choose an album type, set quantity, pick up location, add notes about the photos, and submit. The platform shows live pricing as options are selected.

**Custom Photo Frame**
Select frame size and style, pick quantity and location, add special instructions for the design team.

**Photo Mug**
Order a single personalised mug or bulk corporate mugs. Choose quantity and delivery location.

**Photo Reprint**
Customers who have visited FDCL before can reorder prints using their FDCL Photo ID. Photos can also be uploaded directly from a phone, Google Drive, or Dropbox — no studio visit needed.

### Order Tracking
Every order placed online has a status (pending, in progress, ready, completed) that customers can check from their dashboard at any time.

### Profile Management
Customers can update their name, phone number, email, and password. They can also delete their account permanently if they choose.

## For Staff

### Staff Dashboard
Staff see a dedicated view showing all active orders assigned to their location. They can filter by date, status, or customer name.

### Creating Orders for Walk-in Customers
Staff can place orders on behalf of customers who come in without an online account. The staff order form captures all necessary details — customer info, service type, quantity, and special notes — so the order is logged in the system.

### Managing Existing Orders
Staff can view full order details, update status (e.g. mark as "ready" or "completed"), and add internal notes. They can also edit order details if a customer changes their mind after placing an order.

### Customer Management
Staff have a customer list where they can search for any customer by name or phone number, view their order history, and access contact details.

### Photo Storage
Staff can view and manage the photos stored for each customer. This is linked to the Photo Reprint service — when a customer's photos are on file, staff can quickly retrieve and reprint them.

### Appointments View
All booked appointments appear in a calendar-style list. Staff can see who is coming in, at what time, and for what type of session.

## For Admins

### Admin Dashboard
The admin dashboard gives a bird's-eye view of the entire business. It shows:
- Total orders today, this week, and this month
- Revenue summaries
- Recent activity across both studio locations
- Quick access to all management sections

The dashboard can be filtered by date range and location.

### User Management
Admins can view all registered users (customers and staff), see their account details and order history, deactivate accounts, or delete them if needed.

### Products & Pricing
Admins manage the product catalogue — the albums, frames, mugs, and reprint options that customers can order. For each product, admins set:
- Product name and description
- Price
- Category (album, frame, mug, etc.)
- Which locations offer it

### Reports
The reports section provides a detailed breakdown of orders and revenue over any time period. This helps management understand which services are most popular, which locations are busiest, and how the business is growing.

### Studio Fee Management
Admins can manage the pricing for studio photo sessions (passport, visa, etc.) per location.

## Platform-Wide Features

### Dark Mode / Light Mode
The platform has a theme toggle. Users can switch between a bright light interface and a comfortable dark interface — the preference is saved automatically.

### Bilingual Support (English & Bangla)
The entire customer-facing website supports both English and Bangla. Customers can switch languages in one click. All headings, descriptions, service names, FAQs, and labels appear correctly in both languages.

### Mobile-Friendly Design
The platform is fully responsive — it works on phones, tablets, and desktop computers without any separate app download required.

### Google Sign-In
Customers can register and log in using their existing Google account — no new password to remember.

### Flash Notifications
After any action (placing an order, updating a profile, etc.) the system shows a brief notification confirming success or explaining any error.

### Secure Login with OTP
Phone number verification is required during registration. For sensitive actions, one-time passwords (OTP) sent by SMS add an extra layer of security.
