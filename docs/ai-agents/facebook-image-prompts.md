# Facebook Image Generation Prompts — FDCL

Ready-to-use prompts for AI image generators (Midjourney, DALL·E, Firefly, Ideogram, etc.) to create Facebook post visuals and cover photos for Focus Digital Color Lab.

---

## Brand Visual Guidelines (Apply to All Prompts)

**Color palette:**
- Primary: Deep burgundy / dark red (#8B1A1A range) — the FDCL brand color
- Accents: Warm gold / cream for text and highlights
- Background: Deep charcoal or near-black for premium feel, or clean white for bright variants
- Avoid: Neon colors, flat cartoon styles, heavy gradients

**Style direction:**
- Premium, editorial, warm and professional
- South Asian context where people appear — Bangladeshi/Dhaka setting
- Real studio atmosphere: soft studio lighting, professional backdrop, camera equipment visible
- No stock-photo look — authentic and trustworthy feel

**Text in image:** Do NOT include text in AI image prompts. Add text separately in Canva or similar.

## Tips for Best Results

- **Midjourney:** Add `--ar 4:5` for portrait posts, `--ar 16:9` for cover photos, `--style raw --v 6` for photorealism
- **DALL·E 3:** Prompts above work directly; add "no text, no words, no signs" at the end
- **Adobe Firefly:** Use "Photo" style, set color temperature to warm, detail level to high
- **Ideogram:** Add `[REALISTIC]` tag at the start of each prompt

Always generate 2–4 variants and pick the most authentic-looking one. Avoid images that look like Western stock photos — prioritize South Asian context and faces.
