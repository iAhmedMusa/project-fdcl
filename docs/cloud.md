# Focus Digital Color Lab — Overview

**Focus Digital Color Lab (FDCL)** is a premium photo studio based in Dhaka, Bangladesh, serving customers since 2009. This document explains what the business is, what it offers, and how the online system works — no technical knowledge required.

---

## What Is FDCL?

FDCL is one of Dhaka's most trusted photo studios. For over 15 years, it has helped people get professional photos for passports, visas, school admissions, job applications, and personal keepsakes. The studio is known for its 10-minute express service, professional equipment, and photos accepted by embassies and government offices worldwide.

**At a glance:**
- Founded in 2009
- 50,000+ happy customers
- 5.0 Google rating (500+ reviews)
- 2 studio locations in Dhaka (Bailey Road & Gulshan)
- A third location opening soon in Banasree, Rampura

## How Does the Online System Work?

FDCL has an online platform that connects customers, studio staff, and management in one place. Think of it as the "brain" behind the business.

**Customers** can:
- Create an account and log in
- Book a studio appointment online (pick date, time, location, and type of photo)
- Place orders for albums, frames, mugs, and reprints from home
- Track their order status
- Reorder photos using their personal FDCL Photo ID (no studio visit needed)

**Staff** can:
- See and manage all incoming orders
- Create orders on behalf of walk-in customers
- View customer history and contact details
- Handle photo reprints and uploads

**Admins** can:
- View the full business dashboard — orders, revenue, and activity
- Manage products, pricing, and studio locations
- Handle user accounts (customers and staff)
- View detailed business reports
